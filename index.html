Ledgerleaf

The old bonds in the drawer: what are they worth?

Ledgerleaf is an iPhone app for people who inherited, or simply kept, U.S. savings bonds. Enter each Series EE, I or E bond and see its value today, the interest it earned, and how much of the box sits in bonds that stopped earning.

Nothing leaves your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking, no server of ours. The only outside service is RevenueCat, which records App Store purchases under an anonymous ID.

What it does

Point the camera at a paper bond and Ledgerleaf reads the serial, series, face value and issue date for you to check, or type them in. Every bond gets a timeline: first cash date, end of the early-cash penalty, doubling of an EE bond, the month interest stops, and a marker for today. The main screen adds up the idle money and lists the next dates coming up. Bonds can be grouped by owner, locked with Face ID, and backed up to Files or iCloud Drive.

Where the numbers come from

The U.S. Treasury publishes redemption values every May and November; Ledgerleaf ships them inside the app and is updated when new ones appear. Ledgerleaf is an independent app, not affiliated with the U.S. Department of the Treasury. For electronic bonds, your TreasuryDirect account shows the exact amount.

Cost

The first 3 bonds are free. Ledgerleaf Pro (unlimited bonds, reminders, CSV export) can be bought once, or as a yearly or monthly subscription; prices are shown in the app and vary by country. Subscriptions are managed in your Apple account settings.
