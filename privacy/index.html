Ledgerleaf

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

In short: your bonds stay on your iPhone. Ledgerleaf has no account, advertising or tracking. What leaves the device is the record of an App Store purchase and a few anonymous usage counters, which go to RevenueCat under an anonymous ID.

The bonds you enter

Series, face value or amount paid, issue date, serial number, owner name and note are stored in the app on your iPhone. After each change Ledgerleaf also writes a copy to its own folder, which you can see in the Files app. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. iCloud Backup of your iPhone may include the list, as it does for any app. Deleting a bond removes it; deleting the app removes everything.

Camera and photos

If you scan a bond, the camera or the photo you pick is read by the iPhone's own text recognition, on the device. The photo is shown while you check the fields and is not uploaded or kept. The photo picker only hands over the one photo you choose.

Backups, spreadsheets, Face ID, reminders

A backup or CSV file goes only where you send it from the share sheet. Face ID is handled by iOS: Ledgerleaf learns only whether it succeeded. Reminders are local notifications created on your iPhone.

Purchases

Apple sells and manages Ledgerleaf Pro; we never see your name, email or payment details, and the app only asks Apple whether Pro is active. To keep a reliable record of purchases we use RevenueCat, Inc. as a processor. It receives each in-app purchase you make and stores an anonymous app user ID, the purchase history (product, date, price, transaction IDs) and basic technical data (OS and app version, storefront country). Your bonds, photos and backups are never sent to it. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Usage counters

Through the same RevenueCat library, Ledgerleaf sends a few anonymous counters of product interaction, to know how the Pro screen and the rating request are doing: how many times the Pro screen was shown, how many times it was closed without buying, how many times the app asked for a rating, and in which app version. They are not linked to you, contain nothing you entered (no bonds, owners or photos) and no device identifiers, and are not used to track you. They are not sent in TestFlight or sandbox builds.

To ask for deletion of that purchase record, write to baltajmn@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Ledgerleaf purchase data". Apple keeps its own order records under its own policies.

Everything else

There is no other third-party code in the app. Ledgerleaf works offline; a purchase record is sent when the device next has a connection. The app is not aimed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
